How painting can interfere with  categorization:  

"**It’s suggestive**, **and then your eye can wander and take in space and shape and color without language and narrative and the categorization that ends curiosit**y."

Painting as a liminal space - whether the experience or the act of making it :

I **especially like painting and looking at paintings when I want to quiet that logic part of my brain and open up to new ways of thinking or being without thinking. I go for runs in the morning at 6:30am and it’s still dark these days and the streets are mostly empty (the lone dogwalkers that may pass by are obscured by darkness) and it feels like moving through an interstitial space between the days and between thingness. Like, I dont have to be defined yet and the concerns of the day are still primordial. Anyway, that feels relevant about my experience now and how I see this painting.**

This artists definition of finished painting?  When they were able to draw the viewer to the delicate lilac petal and bright flower lights and soft round texture as part of a **soft soothing space in time not interrupted by forms screaming for attention**

**less is more in the capture of this soft moment**
