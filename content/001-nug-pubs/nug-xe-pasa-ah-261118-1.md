### Divisionism, Caricature and Hyperbole in Process and History

> Exaggeration and hyperbole in art making is profound. In a caricature like that of Daumier or Toulouse-Lautrec the exaggeration is connected to some expression. But in the case of neo-impressionism there is a more abstract idea of enlivening the spirit through a heightening of experience.
> 
> I have been surprised that exaggerating in the process of making art can help unearth the so called right/left brain dichotomy.

*The neo-impressionists are considered the first avant-guard art movement by many prominent art historians.
