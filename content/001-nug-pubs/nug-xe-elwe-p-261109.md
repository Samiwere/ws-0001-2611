### Style  and Process

> I am very curious to  know more about this "sudden" development of a new style? I think it is such an interesting line we can walk as life long learners. There is this moment when we realize that we are separate from what we are learning. It seems that when we are compassionate or impressionable and in a learning process we can get lost in an emulation process. 

>The entire understanding of "style" is culturally and temporally specific. In the Renaissance there were schools and styles but that process in incomparable to a modern 21st century need and search for style.
